Mines Tour Mibladen and Ahouli

Mibladen and Aouli are historic, semi-abandoned mining settlements located in the Upper Moulouya mining district of central Morocco, roughly 25 to 28 kilometers (16 to 17 miles) northeast of Midelt in the Drâa-Tafilalet region.

  • Location & Valley: Situated in a rugged, semi-arid valley along the upper Moulouya River, with Mibladene and Aouli lying about 6 to 10 kilometers (3.7 to 6.2 miles) apart from each other.
  • Industrial History: Developed heavily by French companies like Penarroya and the Compagnie Minière de l'Afrique du Nord starting in the late 1920s and 1930s as major hubs for lead, barite, and metal extraction. Original structures stand as ghost towns today.
  • Mineralogy & Geology: World-renowned among mineral collectors for stunning crystal specimens including vanadinite (bright red crystals), cerussite, galena, barite, wulfenite, fluorite, and secondary copper minerals like azurite.
