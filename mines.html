Industrial History & Minerals

Mines Tour Mibladen and Ahouli

Journey north of Midelt into the dramatic canyon landscapes of the upper Moulouya river valley to discover the legendary abandoned lead mining villages of Mibladen and Ahouli.

  • Historic Stone Structures: Walk among striking, weathered stone and masonry ruins standing as monuments to a bygone industrial era.
  • Arid Mountain Landscapes: Explore structures set against dramatic, sun-drenched valley slopes and rugged geological formations[cite: 4].
  • Mineralogical Heritage: Discover the unique mining heritage renowned globally for brilliant crystals and historical lead extraction[cite: 4].
